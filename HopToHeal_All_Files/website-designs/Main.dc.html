Not sure if your child needs help?

Many children need a little extra support, and spotting it early makes the biggest difference. Tap anything that sounds familiar.

Not talking much yet Doesn't respond to name Clumsy or falls often Upset by noise or touch Finds it hard to sit still Big tantrums Handwriting or school trouble Very picky eating

Not sure? Try the free 10-minute HopCheck. It's a screening, not a diagnosis, and a therapist reviews every result.

How we help

Every plan is built around your child's own challenges, strengths and pace. Sessions feel like play, and you always know why we're doing what we're doing.

Getting started is easy

Four small hops from your first question to steady progress.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your child

    Book online in about 2 minutes, try HopCheck, or just WhatsApp us.

  2. 2

    Meet a therapist

    A friendly, play-based first assessment. You stay with your child the whole time.

  3. 3

    Get a clear plan

    A personal therapy plan, explained in simple words, with goals you can see.

  4. 4

    Grow, hop by hop

    Sessions at the centre, play ideas for home and progress updates on WhatsApp.
