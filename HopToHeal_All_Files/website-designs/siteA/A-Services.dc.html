Occupational therapy

Builds the skills for everyday life: dressing, eating, holding a pencil, playing and joining in at school.

May help if your child: finds buttons, cutting or handwriting hard; tires quickly; avoids messy play.
Ages: 2–16
02

Speech therapy

Helps your child talk, understand others and be understood, through games, songs and everyday words.

May help if your child: uses few words for their age; is hard to understand; struggles to follow instructions.
Ages: 2–16
03

Sensory integration

Swings, textures and movement games that help the body make sense of sound, touch and motion.

May help if your child: is very upset by noise, clothes or food textures; constantly seeks spinning or crashing.
Ages: 2–14
04

Early intervention

Play-based support from the first signs of delay, in the years when the brain changes fastest.

May help if your child: is behind on milestones like walking, talking or playing with others.
Ages: 2–6
05

Special education

One-to-one teaching in reading, writing, numbers and learning skills, at your child's pace.

May help if your child: struggles with reading, spelling or maths despite real effort.
Ages: 4–16
06

Behaviour support & ABA

Positive, structured routines that make daily life calmer for the whole family.

May help if your child: has frequent big tantrums; finds transitions hard; struggles to wait or share.
Ages: 2–16
07

Counselling & parent training

Time for you: practical strategies, so therapy keeps working at home between sessions.

Good for: any parent who wants a plan for home, routines and school conversations.
Ages: parents of 2–16s
08

Group therapy & school readiness

Small groups for turn-taking, friendships and the skills a classroom needs.

May help if your child: finds it hard to play with others or is starting school soon.
Ages: 3–10
