Hoppy chat widget

The floating chatbot from the mascot-chatbot spec, dressed for Hop to Heal. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step: two files drop into any page. It's live on the home page; hover Hoppy, then click.

Three states

How Hoppy moves

Three nested layers, each owning one kind of motion. All pivot at the bottom of the body, so Hoppy rocks like it's standing on the spot.

Button · 92 × 92 px (76 on phones)
Tilt · hover: leans −9°, lifts 4 px, grows to 105% · press: 94%
Sway · rocks −5° to +5° every 3.4 s (5 s while chatting)
Bob · floats up 3 px with a little squash, every 1.7 s
Artwork · rays pulse in turn (2.4 s, 0.25 s apart) · eyes blink every 4.5 s when open
Easy tweaks
  • Swing more: raise --mb-sway-angle to 8deg
  • Calmer: --mb-sway-duration 5s, --mb-bob-height 2px
  • Calm mode and "reduce motion" turn every animation off

Changes from the original
