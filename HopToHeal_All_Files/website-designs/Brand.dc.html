How we move

  • Every animation guides the next step, gives feedback, or is Hoppy. Nothing else moves.
  • Small "hop" on buttons (150 ms), a celebration when a booking or payment is done.
  • No auto-sliding carousels, parallax, flashing or sound that plays by itself.
  • Calm mode in the top bar freezes all motion for sensory-sensitive children, and the site follows the phone's "reduce motion" setting too.
