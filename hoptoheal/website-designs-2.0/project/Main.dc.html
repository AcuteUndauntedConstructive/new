HopCheck · free · about 10 minutes

Two questionnaires. One clear answer.

Grown-ups tell us what they see. Children tell us how it feels. A therapist reads both and matches your family to the right help.

How HopCheck works
For parents and elders The grown-up check 8 areas of your child's day: talking, moving, senses, attention, feelings, friends, learning and daily skills. Plus a few questions about how you are coping. Ages 2–16Hindi or English8 minutes Start as a grown-up For children, 7 and up My turn! Hoppy asks 10 friendly questions, one at a time, with big face buttons and read-aloud. Children share what is hard, and what they are good at. Ages 7–16No typing3 minutes Start the child's turn

HopCheck is a screening, not a diagnosis. Nothing is shown as a result until a qualified therapist has reviewed and signed it.
