What happens to your answers

  1. 1
    Safety first
    If an answer shows a red flag, you see "please see a doctor today" straight away, and a therapist calls you the same day.
  2. 2
    A therapist reads both
    A master's-level therapist reviews the grown-up's and the child's answers together, within 2 working days.
  3. 3
    A signed report
    You see nothing until it is signed. Then: each area in plain words, and the services that fit.
  4. 4
    Your choice
    Book from the report, ask for a free 15-minute call, or do nothing. We check in again in 3 months.

HopCheck is a screening, not a diagnosis. It never names a condition. Only a qualified professional can diagnose after meeting your child.
