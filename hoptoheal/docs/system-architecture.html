Hop to Heal · how the website works

System architecture

One platform behind both website designs: a public site, a parent portal with a section for each child, a therapist portal, booking with live slots, payments, HopCheck screening and shared progress reports. Parents and therapists sign in with Google.

Who uses it

Four kinds of account. A child does not log in; each child is a profile inside a parent's account, and the same child can be linked to two parents.

Parent

Parent or guardian

  • Signs in with Google (phone OTP as backup)
  • Own profile with photo, contact, language
  • Adds children, each with a photo and details
  • Books sessions, buys the 15-day pack, pays by UPI
  • Sees reports, progress and home activities
  • Gets care suggestions written by the therapist
Child profile

Child

  • Name, photo, date of birth, school, languages
  • Concerns, HopCheck results, assessments
  • Goals with improvement levels over time
  • Session history, notes for parents, reports
Therapist

Therapist

  • Signs in with Google; joins by invite from the centre
  • Sees only the children assigned to them
  • Reviews HopCheck results and signs reports
  • Writes session notes, updates goal levels and care tips
  • Sets weekly availability and leave
Centre team

Coordinator and admin

  • Manages bookings, rooms, therapists and centres
  • Handles refunds, invoices and pack credits
  • Assigns children to therapists
  • Admin only: audit log and data requests

Key journeys

1. First sign-in with Google

  1. Parent taps "Continue with Google".
  2. Google returns name, email and photo; the account is created as a parent.
  3. Short setup: phone number for WhatsApp, language, consent.
  4. Add a child: name, date of birth, photo, concerns.
  5. Lands on the parent dashboard with "Book a session" and "Take HopCheck".

Therapists sign in the same way but must use an invite link from the centre, which gives them the therapist role.

2. Therapist updates a child

  1. Opens today's schedule and taps the child.
  2. Marks attendance, writes the session note and a short note for parents.
  3. Moves goal levels (−2 to +2) where progress was seen.
  4. Adds or edits a care tip for the parent.
  5. Parent's dashboard and progress chart update straight away; WhatsApp tells them a note is ready.

3. Booking with live slots and payment

Slots and pricing

Every slot is one hour. A slot can hold as many children as there are free therapists for that service and free rooms. The app shows how many places are left, and holds a place while the parent pays.

places left = min(free therapists for the service, free rooms) − confirmed bookings − active holds (10-minute expiry)

Single session ₹800

One hour with a therapist. Pay when booking, or at the centre for a first visit.

15-day pack · save ₹2,400 ₹9,600

15 one-hour sessions, one a day for 15 days, paid upfront. ₹640 per session instead of ₹800 (20% off).

Pack booking rule: the parent picks a start date and a daily time. The app checks all 15 days; any day where that time is full is shown so the parent can pick another time for that day. Each attended session uses one credit. Still to decide: what happens to unused credits after a missed day, and the cancellation window.

HopCheck screening engine

The full method is in the research paper (HopCheck_Research_Paper.docx). In software terms:

sequenceDiagram
  participant P as Parent
  participant H as HopCheck
  participant T as Therapist (master's level)
  P->>H: Consent, child's age, concerns
  H->>H: Choose the questionnaire for the age band
  P->>H: Answer (10–15 minutes)
  H->>H: Score with versioned rules
  alt Urgent red flag
    H-->>P: Advice to see a doctor today
    H->>T: Same-day call alert
  else Everything else
    H->>T: Add to review queue
  end
  T->>H: Review, call parent if needed, sign
  H-->>P: Report released with next step
PartWhat it does
Instrument libraryEach questionnaire stored as a versioned definition: items, answers, Hindi and English text, age band, licence reference.
Scoring rulesExactly as each manual says; tested against hand-scored examples; a change makes a new version.
Review queueSorted by urgency; target review within 2 working days; red results need a second reviewer.
ReportTraffic light per area, plain words, next step, home activities, reviewer's name and registration.
Progress linkResults become the child's baseline; HopCheck repeats every 3 months for comparison.

Privacy and safety

RuleHow the system meets it
Parental consent before any child data (DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025)Consent screen at sign-up and before HopCheck; version and time stored; withdraw anytime in settings.
No tracking or targeted ads aimed at childrenNo ad pixels or third-party trackers on portal and HopCheck pages; analytics without personal data.
Only the right people see a child's recordRow-level security; therapists see assigned children only; every access written to the audit log.
Photos and reports kept privatePrivate storage; links that expire in minutes; photos never public.
Data stays in IndiaDatabase and storage in the Mumbai region; encrypted at rest and in transit; daily backups.
Parents control their dataDownload all data; delete account; correction requests handled by admin.
Screening, not diagnosisNo automated diagnosis; every recommendation signed by a qualified therapist.
