8. HOPCHECK: THE INNOVATION  [P]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
"A phygital (physical + digital) early-care ecosystem"
Four parts:
  1. HopCheck Screener: a 10-minute online check. Parent questionnaire +
     phone microphone (speech sample) + camera tasks (eye contact, tracing,
     movement). Flags risk in 5 areas. Hindi or English.
  2. School Screening Camps: free camps for ages 5 to 12; parents opt in
     with consent and get a private report.
  3. Hop Home Kit + App: a therapeutic play kit + weekly video activities;
     parents become "co-therapists" and log progress daily.
  4. AI-powered CRM: every lead and child tracked; WhatsApp reminders,
     progress reports and follow-ups sent automatically.
Rule: "Screening, not diagnosis". Every flag is reviewed by a qualified OT.

Child journey (6 steps):
  1 Discover (school camp, Google, Instagram, paediatrician)
  2 Screen (questionnaire + voice + camera, any phone, Hindi/English)
  3 Review (OT reviews flags; TRAFFIC-LIGHT report sent to parent)
  4 Assess & treat (full assessment + personalised plan, centre or home)
  5 Practise at home (Home Kit + app)
  6 Track (CRM shares progress; re-screen every 3 months)

Revenue streams: assessments, therapy packages, home-kit sales + app
  subscription, paid school screening / CSR, licensing HopCheck to clinics.
IP plan: trademark (Hop to Heal, HopCheck, smiling-sun logo, tagline);
  copyright (code, videos, worksheets); design (kit); patent (screening
  method); trade secret (scoring algorithm, data).
